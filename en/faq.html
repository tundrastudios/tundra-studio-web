FAQ

What people ask us most

If your question isn't here, message us on X or by email.

How do I send you my footage?

Through a form where you choose the type of work and add the link to your raw footage (Google Drive, WeTransfer or similar), your extra assets and your instructions.

How long does delivery take?

As a guide: thumbnails in 1 day, shorts in 1 to 2 days and long videos in 3 to 5 days. It depends on the length and what you ask for.

What does the free trial include?

We edit 1 short or 1 thumbnail at no cost, your choice. It doesn't include long videos, and it's one trial per creator.

What if I don't like how it turned out?

Every order includes 2 rounds of revisions so it ends up the way you want.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Packages are month to month, with no long contracts.

What if I don't use my whole package?

The work in your package is for the current month. If you need more, you can order single pieces.

Do you only edit gaming content?

We specialize in gaming and streaming, but if you make other kinds of content, message us and let's talk.
